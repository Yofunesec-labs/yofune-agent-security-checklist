# Code of Conduct

We expect technical disagreement to remain specific, evidence-based, and respectful. Harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory abuse, or deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Security discussions may involve sensitive failure scenarios. Do not publish private customer data, secrets, or exploit details for systems you do not own or have authorization to test.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.
