# Crosswalk — NIST AI RMF / Generative AI Profile

NIST AI RMF is used here as a governance and risk-management lens rather than as a one-to-one security-control catalog. Control-level records map to the AI RMF core functions **GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE**.

As of the 2026-09-18 snapshot, NIST states that AI RMF 1.0 is being revised. NIST AI 600-1 (Generative AI Profile) remains a cross-sectoral companion profile. YASC therefore keeps this relationship as a dated mapping rather than mirroring NIST structure.

| YASC emphasis | Typical AI RMF function |
|---|---|
| ownership, inventory, policy, lifecycle | GOVERN |
| system scope, context, trust boundaries, risk identification | MAP |
| testing, telemetry, evaluation, evidence | MEASURE |
| authorization, isolation, approval, containment, remediation | MANAGE |
